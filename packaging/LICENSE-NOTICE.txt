=================================================

1. AUTHORISED USE ONLY
----------------------

AETHRA-SEC must only be operated against networks, hosts and interfaces that
you are explicitly authorised to monitor or assess.

It performs no exploitation, no credential attacks and no automated mitigation.
Every mitigation output is advisory and requires a human decision.

Running port scans or packet capture against networks you do not administer, or
do not have written permission to test, is unlawful in most jurisdictions.
Installing this software does not grant you that permission, and by continuing
you accept responsibility for obtaining it.

AETHRA-SEC is intended for academic Network Security laboratories and
authorised internal LAN environments.

5. DATA HANDLING
----------------

AETHRA-SEC records what it actually observes on the network you point it at.
Captured traffic metadata, discovered hosts, alerts and incidents are stored in
your own MySQL database on your own machine. Nothing is transmitted to the
authors or to any third party.

Passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes. Database and SMTP credentials are
held in a configuration file whose permissions are restricted to Administrators
and SYSTEM.
