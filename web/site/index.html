Scope, stated plainly

What it does — and what it will never do.

Targets outside your configured authorised networks are refused before any command is even built.

It does
  • Runs authorised Nmap assessments from a fixed profile catalogue.
  • Captures and analyses live traffic on an interface you select.
  • Receives real Snort alerts over four configurable transports.
  • Correlates evidence into incidents with a transparent risk breakdown.
  • Recommends mitigation and explains its reasoning.
  • Records every action in a hash-chained audit trail.
It never does
  • Exploit anything — no Metasploit, no attack tooling.
  • Attempt credential guessing or brute force.
  • Reconfigure a firewall, block an IP, or disable a service.
  • Invent packets, alerts, hosts or statistics.
  • Apply a mitigation on its own — a human always decides.
  • Store a plaintext password, or email one out.
